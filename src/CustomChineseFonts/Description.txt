[h1]Custom Chinese Fonts[/h1]

Custom Simplified Chinese UI fonts for Oxygen Not Included. Body text defaults to HarmonyOS Sans SC; titles can be switched in Settings, and you can drop in your own fonts. This mod uses HarmonyOS Sans Fonts.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]Features[/h3]

[list]
[*]Chinese body text uses HarmonyOS Sans SC Regular by default.
[*]Chinese titles default to HarmonyOS Sans SC Bold; Jinzishe Dezheng and your own font are optional in Settings.
[*]English fonts are left unchanged by default. Enable English replacement in Settings to swap GRAYSTROKE / Roboto.
[/list]

[h3]Settings[/h3]

[list]
[*]Open the Mods list from the main menu or pause menu, then click Settings on this mod's row.
[*]After turning English replacement off, fully restart the game to restore vanilla English fonts.
[/list]

[h3]Your own fonts[/h3]

[list]
[*]Put a TTF or OTF in [i]Documents/Klei/OxygenNotIncluded/mods/config/CustomChineseFonts/custom_fonts/[/i]
[*][i]body.ttf[/i] or [i]body.otf[/i] replaces Chinese body text
[*][i]title.ttf[/i] or [i]title.otf[/i] adds a Custom title option in Settings
[*]The folder (and a [i]README.txt[/i]) is created the first time the mod loads
[*]Fully quit the game after adding or replacing fonts
[*]You must have the right to use the font. Missing glyphs fall back to HarmonyOS Sans SC
[/list]

[h1]自定义中文字体 / Custom Chinese Fonts[/h1]

[i]自定义缺氧游戏里的中文界面字体。正文默认 HarmonyOS Sans SC，标题可在设置里切换，也可以放进自己的字体。本模组使用 HarmonyOS Sans Fonts。[/i]

这是一个 Oxygen Not Included 中文界面字体模组。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]功能[/h3]

[list]
[*]中文[b]正文[/b]默认使用 HarmonyOS Sans SC Regular。
[*]中文[b]标题[/b]默认 HarmonyOS Sans SC Bold，可在设置里改成金字社得正体，或你自己的字体。
[*][b]英文字体[/b]默认不替换；可在设置里打开，用当前正文字体 / 标题字体替换 GRAYSTROKE / Roboto。
[/list]

[h3]设置[/h3]

[list]
[*]主菜单或暂停菜单打开模组列表，在本模组那一行点「设置」。
[*]关闭英文替换后，需要完全重启游戏才会恢复原版英文字体。
[/list]

[h3]用自己的字体[/h3]

[list]
[*]把 TTF 或 OTF 放到 [i]Documents/Klei/OxygenNotIncluded/mods/config/CustomChineseFonts/custom_fonts/[/i]
[*][i]body.ttf[/i] 或 [i]body.otf[/i]：替换中文正文
[*][i]title.ttf[/i] 或 [i]title.otf[/i]：出现在设置里的「自定义」标题选项
[*]第一次启动模组后，这个文件夹会自动创建，里面有一份 [i]README.txt[/i]
[*]放好或更换字体后，必须[b]完全退出游戏再进[/b]（热重载无效）
[*]请确保你有权使用该字体。缺字时会回退到鸿蒙黑体
[/list]
