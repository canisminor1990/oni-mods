[h1]Thermal Info Cards[/h1]

Shows thermal conductivity, thermal mass, heat energy, and phase-change temperatures on hover and in the build menu. Hover cards can stack matching objects and sum thermal mass and heat energy. Peter Han's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1983504552]Thermal Tooltips[/url] and Aze's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1960947963]Better Info Cards[/url] conflict when both are enabled; this remake exists to combine both features so you do not need either original or PLib.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/oni-mods/refs/heads/master/compatibility.png[/img]

[h3]Languages[/h3]

UI text covers English, Chinese, Japanese, and Korean.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/oni-mods/refs/heads/master/i18n.png[/img]

[h3]Features[/h3]

[list]
[*]Hovering a building, debris, or tile shows temperature, thermal conductivity, thermal mass, and heat energy.
[*]Matching hover cards stack; titles, germs, ore mass, temperature, thermal mass, and heat energy are summed or averaged.
[*]Click cycles through stacked cards. Shift+click cycles objects inside a stack.
[*]Phase changes list the result (and any secondary ore) at the real transition temperature, including the 3 °C hysteresis.
[*]The build menu material pane shows the finished building's conductivity, thermal mass, and melting point for the selected material.
[*]Insulated tiles and non-solid buildings use the same mass and conductivity rules as the game's heat simulation.
[/list]

[h3]Settings[/h3]

[list]
[*]Open the Mods list, then click Settings on this mod.
[*][b]Display All Units[/b]: show Celsius, Fahrenheit, and Kelvin together. The game's preferred unit is listed first.
[*][b]Only on Thermal Overlay[/b]: extra stats appear only on the Temperature Overlay. Temperature itself still shows in the default view.
[*][b]Hide Element Categories[/b], [b]Restrict Selections[/b], [b]First Selection Hover[/b], and [b]Compact Cards[/b] control the stacked hover cards.
[*]Thermal lines apply immediately. Card size and some overlay tweaks may need a restart.
[/list]

[h3]Notes[/h3]

[list]
[*]Please do not enable this together with the original [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1983504552]Thermal Tooltips[/url] or [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1960947963]Better Info Cards[/url].
[*]Display All Temps keeps control of the temperature string if that mod is present.
[/list]

[h1]热量信息卡 / Thermal Info Cards[/h1]

[i]原版 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1983504552]Thermal Tooltips[/url] 与 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=1960947963]Better Info Cards[/url] 互相冲突、无法同时启用。本模组把两者合并，在悬浮提示和建造菜单里显示热导率、热容、热量和相变温度；相同物体的卡片会合并并合计热容/热量。用语按[url=https://oxygennotincluded.wiki.gg/zh/wiki/%E8%AE%A1%E9%87%8F%E5%8D%95%E4%BD%8D#%E7%83%AD%E9%87%8F]计量单位 · 热量[/url]。独立制作，无需原版或 PLib。[/i]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/oni-mods/refs/heads/master/compatibility.png[/img]

[h3]语言[/h3]

界面文本覆盖英语、中文、日语和韩语。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/canisminor1990/oni-mods/refs/heads/master/i18n.png[/img]

[h3]功能[/h3]

[list]
[*]悬停建筑、掉落物或地格时，显示温度、热导率、热容和热量。
[*]相同悬浮卡片会叠在一起；名称、病菌、矿石质量、温度、热容和热量会合计或取平均。
[*]点击在叠卡之间切换。按住 Shift 再点可在同一叠里的物体之间切换。
[*]相变会列出产物（以及次要矿石）和实际转变温度，已计入 3 °C 滞后。
[*]建造菜单的材料面板会显示完工后的热导率、热容和熔点。
[*]隔热砖和非实体建筑使用与游戏热模拟相同的质量与热导规则。
[/list]

[h3]设置[/h3]

[list]
[*]打开模组列表，点击本模组的「设置」。
[*][b]显示全部温度单位[/b]：同时显示摄氏度、华氏度和开尔文（绝对温标），游戏首选单位排在最前。
[*][b]仅在温度视图下显示[/b]：热导率、热容和热量只在温度视图出现。默认视图仍显示温度。
[*][b]隐藏元素类别[/b]、[b]限制选择范围[/b]、[b]首次选择指向对象[/b]、[b]紧凑卡片[/b] 控制叠卡行为。
[*]热量行立即生效。卡片尺寸和部分视图改动可能需要重启。
[/list]
