[h1]More Room Types[/h1]

Adds extra colony room types. This standalone mod is built with thanks on pether.pg's [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2348079481]Rooms Expanded[/url]; you do not need to subscribe to the original.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]New rooms[/h3]

[list]
[*][b]Private Bathroom[/b]: exactly one flush toilet, one sink, and one shower, with decor and a finished back wall (12–32 tiles). Grants the same morale bonus as a Washroom, and showers finish 20% faster.
[*][b]Warehouse[/b]: at least 4 storage buildings (24–120 tiles). Classification only; storage capacity is unchanged.
[*][b]Battery Room[/b]: at least 8 batteries (24–120 tiles). Rocket battery modules count. Batteries in the room lose charge more slowly.
[*][b]Oxygen Room[/b]: an oxygen producer plus a gas pump (12–64 tiles). Matching equipment runs 10% more efficiently.
[*][b]Waste Processing Room[/b]: Compost plus a Sludge Press or Fertilizer Synthesizer (12–64 tiles). Matching processors run 10% more efficiently.
[*][b]Water Treatment Room[/b]: Water Sieve or Desalinator (12–64 tiles). Matching equipment runs 10% more efficiently.
[*][b]Nuclear Power Plant[/b]: Research Reactor plus a Steam Turbine (24–120 tiles). Grants the same bonus as a Power Plant: generators can be tuned with microchips. Takes priority over Power Plant.
[*][b]Hallway[/b]: Ladder and Fire Pole in the same enclosed space (12–240 tiles). Low priority so it does not override functional rooms. Duplicants gain Athletics while inside.
[/list]

[h3]From Rooms Expanded[/h3]

With thanks to pether.pg, the following rooms from the original mod are still included here:

[list]
[*][b]Gym Room[/b]: Manual Generator, Water Cooler, and a decorative item (12–64 tiles). Working the generator trains Athletics faster. Power Plant still takes priority.
[*][b]Art Museum[/b]: Pedestal, 6 masterpieces, and a displayed ornament (32–120 tiles). Visiting grants morale from Creativity (up to +10). Upgrades to Space Museum.
[*][b]Space Museum[/b]: Pedestal plus 6 unique artifacts (32–120 tiles). Visiting grants morale from Piloting, with extra morale after Cosmic Archaeology.
[*][b]Graveyard[/b]: Tasteful Memorial and a wild plant, no industrial machinery (12–96 tiles). Visiting can raise or lower stress for a fraction of a cycle.
[*][b]Industrial Room[/b]: Any industrial machinery (12–96 tiles). Low-priority classification that upgrades into Power Plant, Farm, Stable, Gym, or the utility rooms above.
[/list]

[h3]Notes[/h3]

[/list]

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]新增房间[/h3]

[list]
[*][b]私人卫生间[/b]：恰好 1 个抽水马桶、1 个洗手池与 1 个淋浴间，需要装饰与后墙（12–32 格）。加成与官方卫生间相同，淋浴时间缩短 20%。
[*][b]仓库[/b]：至少 4 个储物建筑（24–120 格）。仅房间分类，不改容量。
[*][b]储能室[/b]：至少 8 个电池（24–120 格）。火箭电池仓也算。室内电池自放电更慢。
[*][b]制氧室[/b]：制氧设备 + 气泵（12–64 格）。相关设备效率提升 10%。
[*][b]废料处理间[/b]：堆肥堆 + 泥浆压榨器或肥料合成器（12–64 格）。相关设备效率提升 10%。
[*][b]水处理间[/b]：净水器或脱盐器（12–64 格）。相关设备效率提升 10%。
[*][b]核电站[/b]：研究反应堆 + 蒸汽涡轮机（24–120 格）。加成与官方发电厂相同：可用电控站微芯片调校发电机。优先于发电厂。
[*][b]走廊[/b]：同一封闭空间内同时有梯子和消防滑杆（12–240 格）。低优先级，不会覆盖功能房间。复制人在走廊内获得运动加成。
[/list]

[h3]来自 Rooms Expanded[/h3]

感谢 pether.pg，以下房间沿用自原模组：

[list]
[*][b]健身房[/b]：人力发电机、饮水机与一件装饰物（12–64 格）。踩发电机时运动技能涨得更快。发电厂仍优先于健身房。
[*][b]艺术博物馆[/b]：基座、6 件杰作与展示装饰（32–120 格）。参观按创造力给予士气（最高 +10）。可升级为太空博物馆。
[*][b]太空博物馆[/b]：基座 + 6 件独特文物（32–120 格）。参观按驾驶给予士气；完成「宇宙考古」后还有额外士气。
[*][b]墓地[/b]：雅致的纪念碑与一株野生植物，不能有工业器械（12–96 格）。参观可能升高或降低压力。
[*][b]车间[/b]：任意工业器械（12–96 格）。低优先级分类，可升级为发电厂、农场、畜舍、健身房或上方的功能房间。
[/list]
