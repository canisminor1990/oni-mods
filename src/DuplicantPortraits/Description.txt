[h1]Duplicant Portraits[/h1]

Adds a masterpiece portrait of every duplicant personality to the vanilla 2x2 Blank Canvas.

This is only a cosmetic mod, gameplay is unaffected.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]Features[/h3]

[list]
[*]No new building. Portraits use the smallest vanilla canvas.
[*]One painting per loaded personality, using the built-in headshot.
[*]After an artist finishes a masterpiece, pick it from the vanilla artwork selector: "Portrait of {Name}".
[*]Includes personalities from subscribed DLCs.
[/list]

[h3]How to use[/h3]

[olist]
[*]Build a Blank Canvas.
[*]Have a duplicant with Masterworks decorate it.
[*]Select the painting and choose "Portrait of {Name}".
[/olist]

Decor matches vanilla masterpiece paintings. The portrait is the built-in personality art, not a live shot of colony outfits.

[h1]复制人头像画 / Duplicant Portraits[/h1]

[i]为每个复制人性格增加一幅杰作肖像，挂在原版 2x2 空白画布上。[/i]

仅装饰，不影响玩法。不新增建筑。

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Sgt-Imalas/Sgt_Imalas-Oni-Mods/refs/heads/master/Compat_All.png[/img]

[h3]功能[/h3]

[list]
[*]使用原版最小的空白画布（2x2）。
[*]每个已加载的性格一幅，使用游戏自带的头像。
[*]艺术家完成后，用原版选画界面选择「某某的肖像」。
[*]包含当前已启用的 DLC 性格。
[/list]

[h3]用法[/h3]

[olist]
[*]建造空白画布。
[*]让有「杰作」技能的复制人完成装饰。
[*]选中画作，选择「某某的肖像」。
[/olist]

装饰值与原版杰作相同。画像不会显示殖民地当前的服装。
